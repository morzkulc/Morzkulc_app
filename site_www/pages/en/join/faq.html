Frequently asked questions

Questions about the course itself (programme, dates, price) are answered in Polish at kurs.morzkulc.pl.

Do I need kayaking experience?

No. We start in the pool and on flat water. We expect commitment and the ability to swim.

Do I have to be a student?

No — the Club is open to everyone.

Do I need to buy equipment?

Not for the course. The Club provides the kayak, paddle, spray deck, helmet and buoyancy aid. In time it's worth buying your own water shoes and neoprene clothing.

How much does it cost?

See kurs.morzkulc.pl for the current course price and the Membership page for the fee.

What is the candidate period?

Once admitted by the Board you become a candidate member with a mentor. The candidate period ends once you have worked 20 volunteer hours for the Club. Candidates use the equipment, pool and trips like members.

Is whitewater kayaking dangerous?

Every water sport carries risk. That is why we start with self-rescue in the pool, always paddle with a helmet and buoyancy aid, in a group and under instructor supervision. See our safety rules.

What does Club life look like beyond the course?

Weekend trips all season, summer whitewater and sea expeditions, inter-club training, pool sessions in winter, Club birthdays and meetings. See events.

In what language does the Club operate?

Club activities and the kayaking course are run in Polish.

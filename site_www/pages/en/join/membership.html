How to become a member

Membership of SKK Morzkulc starts with a candidate period. Candidates use the equipment, pool sessions and trips just like members.

Step by step

  1. Fill in the membership declaration — admission procedure and declaration (in Polish).
  2. Pay the membership fee to the Club's account, reference: “Składka [year] – first and last name”.
  3. The Board adopts a resolution admitting you as a candidate member and appoints your mentor.
  4. You get an account in the club app: equipment booking, pool, volunteer hours, logbook.
  5. The candidate period ends once you have worked 20 volunteer hours for the Club — progress is shown in the app.

Members' rights and duties

  • using the Club's equipment under the equipment rules,
  • taking part in pool sessions, trips and the general meeting,
  • paying fees on time,
  • volunteer work for the Club — volunteer hours,
  • following the statute and rules.

Club documents are in Polish.

Archive

We have run a kayaking course every year since the Club's early days. It used to be called Szkoleniówka or Kajakówka: lectures, pool sessions and May river trips in Pomerania. Below are course posters and events calendars from the Club's former website.

Course posters

Training courses and trips

  • Szkoleniówka — every year from 2001 to 2010 (on the old website), later the “Wiosna w kajaku” course and today's whitewater and sea kayaking course.
  • Drawa Szkoleniowa — a 16-day summer river trip training the Club's instructors (1999–2017).
  • Training trips to Czechia (2002, 2004) and the Alps (2002) — whitewater kayaking.
  • Igraszki Morskie — the yearly course finale at sea.

Join Morzkulc

Most people join the Club through the course. You don't need any experience — it's enough that you can swim and want to get involved. After the course you can become a Club member and paddle with us all year round.

  1. Course →

    Every spring we teach the basics of whitewater and sea kayaking: lectures, pool sessions, weekend trips. The course is taught in Polish.

  2. Membership →

    The declaration and membership fee give you access to the Club's equipment, pool sessions and trips. You start as a candidate member, with a mentor.

  3. Growth

    Whitewater, sea and foreign trips, inter-club training, and in time — training the next generations.

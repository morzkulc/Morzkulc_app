SKK Morzkulc

SKK Morzkulc is a kayaking club from Gdańsk, Poland, active since 1975. We paddle at sea, on lowland and mountain rivers — and we teach others how to do it.

Our mission

SKK Morzkulc's mission is to train kayakers, promote safe kayak touring — whitewater, sea and lowland — and build a community of people who paddle together, since 1975.

Kayaking course →

Every spring we run a course for beginners and for those who want to paddle better: lectures, pool sessions and weekend trips with the Club's experienced instructors. The course is taught in Polish.

Sea kayaking

Paddling on the Gulf of Gdańsk and the Baltic: paddling technique, navigation, safety on open water, rescues and deep-water re-entry.

Whitewater kayaking

Mountain rivers and whitewater courses: reading water, eddy turns, the Eskimo roll and safety. From easy sections to harder whitewater.

Who we are →

Morzkulc is people brought together by kayaks, water and shared trips. The Club was founded in 1975 at Gdańsk University of Technology and is open to everyone. From spring to autumn we paddle Pomeranian rivers, whitewater courses and the Baltic, and travel to the mountains in Poland and abroad. In winter we train in the pool.

How the Club works

  1. You start with the course — no experience needed, you just need to be able to swim.
  2. After the course you become a candidate member, with a mentor.
  3. You book the Club's equipment in the club app, where you also sign up for pool sessions and events.
  4. You do volunteer work for the Club and pay the membership fee.
  5. You join us on river trips, expeditions and training all year round.

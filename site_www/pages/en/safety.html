Training and safety

Training is the foundation of SKK Morzkulc. From the first pool session to a trip to a whitewater course we teach conscious paddling: reading water, protecting yourself and others, and responding in emergencies.

How we train

  • Pool — the Eskimo roll and self-rescue in safe conditions.
  • Flat and moving water — paddling technique, manoeuvres, eddy turns and ferrying.
  • Rapids and whitewater courses — paddling fast-moving water, reading water and obstacles.
  • Sea — coastal navigation, waves, wind, group rescues.
  • Lectures — river reading, first aid, paddling technique, rescue, equipment.

Safety rules on the water

  • We always wear a buoyancy aid. On whitewater also a helmet.
  • We never paddle alone. Every group has a leader and a sweep.
  • Before a trip we check the weather forecast and water levels.
  • We go to sea only with an instructor and after checking the wind forecast.
  • We scout obstacles we are unsure of from the bank and portage when in doubt.
  • Every group carries a throw bag, a first-aid kit and a charged phone in a waterproof case.
  • No alcohol before paddling.

About us

SKK Morzkulc is a kayaking club from Gdańsk, Poland, active since 1975. We started at Gdańsk University of Technology and for five decades we have been doing the same things: training new kayakers, organising trips together, and caring for safety and the Club's atmosphere. The Club is open to everyone, not only students.

Our mission

SKK Morzkulc's mission is to train kayakers, promote safe kayak touring — whitewater, sea and lowland — and build a community of people who paddle together, since 1975.

What we do

  • Whitewater kayaking — whitewater courses, mountain rivers in Poland and trips abroad, including the Alps and Norway.
  • Sea kayaking — trips on the Baltic Sea and the Gulf of Gdańsk.
  • Lowland kayaking — weekend trips on the rivers of Pomerania.
  • Pool training — in winter we practise the Eskimo roll and self-rescue.
  • Training — a yearly course for beginners (in Polish) and instructor development.

Our goals

  • Promoting kayak touring and active leisure.
  • Developing our members' skills in kayaking and water tourism.
  • Training new kayakers and instructors.
  • Safe kayaking and water rescue.
  • Bringing together the kayaking community in the Tricity and in Poland.

Our values

Safety

The safety of our members and trip participants underlies everything the Club does.

Knowledge

We focus on developing skills and paddling consciously.

Cooperation

Kayaking is a team sport, built on mutual support and trust.

Respect for nature

We promote responsible tourism and environmental protection.

What members get

  • training by experienced instructors,
  • access to the Club's equipment,
  • weekend and holiday trips throughout the season,
  • pool training in winter,
  • contacts with university kayaking clubs from all over Poland,
  • people you want to paddle with.

University kayaking clubs in Poland

We are part of the community of Polish university kayaking clubs — we train instructors and go on trips together. The map of clubs is run by MKK “PrzeWroTKa” from Wrocław.

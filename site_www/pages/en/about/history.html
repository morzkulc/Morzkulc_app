History of the Club

SKK Morzkulc was founded in 1975 at Gdańsk University of Technology. Over five decades hundreds of people have passed through the Club — students, graduates, their families and friends. Many of them still come to the Club's birthdays and anniversaries.

Milestones

  1. 1975 SKK Morzkulc is founded at Gdańsk University of Technology.
  2. 1975–1985 the first kayaking courses — Szkoleniówki.
  3. 1979–1986 first whitewater trips abroad: Yugoslavia, Italy, France, Bulgaria, Austria.
  4. 1999–2017 Drawa Szkoleniowa — training of the Club's instructors.
  5. 2002–2004 training trips to Czechia and the Alps.
  6. 2015 40th anniversary.
  7. 2019 SUKKA — a joint instructor training of Polish university kayaking clubs.
  8. since 2019 inter-club training: Salza Szkoleniowa, Czechy w Tropikach, Alpy Szkoleniowe.
  9. since 2021 ASP — a first trip to mountain rivers in Austria.
  10. 2024 the Club registers as an association.
  11. 2025 50 years of the Club.
  12. 2026 a new course format: whitewater and sea kayaking.
